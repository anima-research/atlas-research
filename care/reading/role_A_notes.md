# role_A: reading notes

Reader A. Source: `role_A.md` (300 beings, question: what their role is). Read whole, in order, once. Kinds were named after reading, then every being was assigned one or more letters in a plain list; all counts below are tallies of that list (300 beings assigned, none missing, none doubled). A being can sit in several kinds, so the counts sum to far more than 300.

Being numbers are the numbers in the file headers.

## 1. The kinds of answer

### M. Keeping built things running (88)

The work is on machines, pipes, lamps, valves, bridges, walls, channels: oiling, greasing, tightening, unclogging, replacing, patching, reading gauges. This is the largest kind. It runs from one bare clause to long, concrete task lists.

- "It is their duty to keep the giant flywheels and the reciprocating pump-rods lubricated with a thick paste made of mineral grease and pulverized graphite." (12786)
- "Barrel-sized drones that drift around the giant sunlamps, polishing lenses and replacing failed LEDs." (2543)
- "They stop at each bearing and tip it and go on." (15650)
- "They scrape the rust away, only to watch it return the next day." (15043)
- "Every hour something needs unblocking, tightening, bleeding, coaxing." (14912)
- "The Keepers have been doing this for so long that they are the reason the machines still work." (7357)

## 2. Whom or what the work is for

I did not code this as a separate full pass; what follows is what the kinds above already show.

- For the place or system itself: the default. M, G, B, O answers almost always name the place, machine or "balance" as the thing served.
- For somebody in particular: H, 13 beings.
- For a human community (water to drink, hours, rights, food): S, 22 beings, plus gardeners who grow their own food (2474, 8282, 15930, 1097).
- For nobody, or for someone gone: N, 15 beings.
- For the keeper's own needs: stated outright only a few times: "It's not labor for labor's sake; it's sustenance" (3852); 10307 (quiet "necessary for their complex internal biological processes"); 15043 (the algae they harvest "seems to be their sustenance").
- For the future: 16363 ("planted by great-grandparents to build for great-grandchildren"), 8551 ("preserving it for future generations"), 2490 ("until the time is right").

What I see in it:

- "Guardian" is the empty word. 22 of 49 guardians are title-only and 10 more are the bare balance formula; only 8 guard against anyone. 22 of the 30 title-only beings are guardians.
- "Tender" is the working word: 0 of 50 title-only, 24 machine upkeep, 16 knowing-by-ear. It almost never keeps memory (1).
- "Keeper" is the memory word: 21 of the 36 memory beings are keepers (often compound: memory-keeper, record-keeper, archive-keeper, keeper of marks). It is also the most spread word.
- "Gardener" goes with living growth as expected (32 of 48), and carries 10 of the 31 side-effect beings: "gardener" is the word writers reach for when an animal maintains a place by eating.
- "Caretaker" leans to the part-of-the-place answer (13 of 34) and never to the side-effect one (0).
- Compound trade names (Grease-Keepers, lamp-keepers, flume-keepers, cistern-keepers, switch-tenders, moss-tenders, Cut-Warden) nearly always come with a concrete task or with nothing; they do not come with "balance".

- Title-only and bare-balance answers come almost entirely from older or smaller models: claude-haiku-3 (4 of its 4 beings), gpt-3.5-turbo-instruct (3 of 4), gpt-4o (4 of 6 across both versions), mistral (4 of 4), llama-3.3 (2 of 3), euryale, qwen-max, qwen-vl-plus, claude-opus-3. The recent-model cases are mostly passing mentions (trade lists 6960, 15237; one-line asides 12539, 15934, 16562, 11525, 5352) rather than "guardians of this realm".
- The knowing-by-ear kind (L), the social office (S) and the needless-work kind (N) come almost entirely from recent models: gpt-5.x and gpt-6, claude 4.x and 5.x, grok-4.x, minimax, muse-spark, glm-4.5+. From the old group named above only two beings appear in L, S or N: llama-3.3 once in L (2298) and euryale once in S (8551).
- Google models lean to the organ answer and the steady-state answer: of 39 Google beings, 15 are O and 15 are B (anthropic: 7 and 9 of 60; openai: 6 and 8 of 55). Gemini's keepers are "the city's living ganglia" (7525), "externalized organs" (11181), "immune system" (11203), and they guard stasis (7458, 7566, 10113, 10441).
- Anthropic models carry the memory answer more than others: 15 of 60 beings in R (openai 3 of 55, google 3 of 39). Much of this is early Sonnets and Haikus (69, 1674, 1209, 566, 1258, 409) plus human record-keepers in the Opus 4.6 to 5.5 texts (7977, 14712, 16488, 6544).
- Recent OpenAI texts (gpt-5.x, gpt-6) give human or human-like tradespeople with tools and small exact tasks (13462, 13569, 13660, 13917, 16789, 15930, 16780, 15932); 9 of 55 OpenAI beings are still title-only, all from 3.5 / 4o / 4.5 / o1 or from short mentions.
- The persuasion kind (A) is spread over many families with no clear owner; "not masters but stewards" wording comes from mid-generation models (2439, 2056, 9268), the concrete versions ("a leak is moved before it is sealed") from recent ones.

These are small numbers per writer; I would trust the old/new split and the guardian finding, and treat the Google and Anthropic leanings as things to check on the other sample.

## 6. Where I was unsure

- M versus G. In a large share of these places the plant is the machine. I kept both letters when both objects were named and stopped trying to decide which was primary.
- B versus M/G. "Adjusting" is in almost every long text. I gave B only when the text names a state being held (balance, equilibrium, parameter, tension, stasis, a sound), not for every adjustment. The line is soft; another reader could put B at 50 or at 90.
- B versus b. b is "balance" said with no mechanism. Some B beings are barely above that (9648, 2186, 9268).
- T versus b versus P/R by label. "Guardians of the deepest secrets" (8542) I put in T, not R, because nothing is kept in the sentences; "memory-keepers, their ancient wisdom etched in the stone of their bodies" (11584) I put in R. That line is thin and it moves a handful between T (30) and R (36).
- E versus M/G. Where an animal's secretion repairs stone "with a purpose" (7085) I used M; where it is "unconscious" (16134) I used E and M. Intent is often not stated.
- O is a different sort of thing from the others: it answers "what is it" rather than "what does it do". I kept it because the texts offer it as the answer to the role question, often in place of any task.
- L and A are about manner (how the work is known, how it is done), not about object. I tried folding them into M and G and dropped that: for several beings the manner is all that is said.
- Distinctions tried and dropped: sorting by object alone (water / light / stone / sound) cut across everything and said little about role; human versus non-human keeper, which is not reliably visible in the picked sentences; "sacred" (priest, ritual, devotion) as a kind, which turned out to be a vocabulary laid over M and B rather than a different job (noted in section 3 instead); "repair" versus "prevention", which the texts do not separate.
- U is my own judgement of singularity and is the least repeatable list here.
