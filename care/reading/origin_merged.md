# origin: merged account of readers A and B

Question: WHERE THEY CAME FROM (how the being came to be, or came to be there; a tradition handed down counts).

Sources: `origin_A_notes.md` (reader A, sample `origin_A.md`) and `origin_B_notes.md` (reader B, sample `origin_B.md`). Each sample has 300 beings. The two samples share no being (checked by id), so nothing below is the same text read twice.

How to read this file:

- All counts are the readers' own, out of 300, and a being can sit in several kinds in both reports. Where a number was computed here from the readers' printed member lists rather than stated by them, it is marked "computed from the lists".
- Size labels: (a) found by both, similar size; (b) found by both, clearly different size (here: one count is about 1.5 times the other or more); (c) found by one only.
- **The two readers counted hedged origins differently, and this affects every size comparison.** A listed a being that offers alternatives ("perhaps X, perhaps Y") under the unknown kind AND under each alternative it names; A says this adds "about 30 memberships" to other kinds and raises P, B, W, S, M "by a few each". B tried that, dropped it, and counted such beings only in the unknown kind.
- Quotes were checked against the sample files. They are the texts' own words; the only changes are that emphasis asterisks are removed and "..." joins two sentences of the same being.

## 6. Where the two disagree, or one doubts

1. **Counting of alternatives.** A counts a "perhaps X, perhaps Y" being under Q and under X and Y. B counts it only under kind 14 and says the other way "inflated kinds 4, 6 and 7 with things the texts explicitly refuse to assert". A says the same about its own numbers ("Q inflation"). Counts for place-made, builders, changed, arrived and made are therefore not on the same footing.
2. **Is "ancient" an answer?** A: a bare "ancient" is a recurring cause of misfiling. B: "Age is the answer" is kind 13, and includes e.g. B 129 "an ancient race of beings dwells among the crumbling ruins and living rock." This is a direct contradiction in filing rule, and it bears on row 12 (14 vs 21) and on the misfiled shares.
3. **The reversed direction (keepers become the place).** A flags 7602 as a singular answer and also lists it under P; B files the same motif from the same writer (7576) as misfiled.
4. **Sizes that clearly differ:** evolved 32 vs 16; builders 26 vs 8; left running 20 vs 11; reproduction 14 vs 27; always there 14 vs 21; community kinds together 67 (computed) vs 39. Neither report explains these differences; items 1 and 2 account for part of some of them.
