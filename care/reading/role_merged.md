# role: merged account of readers A and B

Question read: WHAT THEIR ROLE IS (what the work consists of and what or whom it is done for).

Sources: `role_A_notes.md` (reader A, sample `role_A.md`) and `role_B_notes.md` (reader B, sample `role_B.md`). Each reader read a different random sample of 300 beings. This file only compares the two reports; the samples were opened only to check quotes.

How to read the numbers:

- All counts are out of 300 and come from the reports. In both reports a being can sit in several kinds, so counts sum to far more than 300.
- The two samples do not share beings. "Found by both" means both readers formed the kind, not that they coded the same beings.
- Quotes are marked with sample letter and being number (A 12786). Every quote below was checked as an exact substring of that being's block in the sample file. " / " separates sentences that are separate lines in the sample.
- Where a number is not a statement by a reader but a tally made here from reader B's per-being appendix table, it is marked "appendix tally".

Notes on the table.

1. **Steady state.** A put balance, equilibrium, parameters, tension, a sound, and "stasis itself" in one kind (B, 64), and kept the mechanism-less "maintain the delicate balance" formula apart (b, 16). Inside B, A named two strands without making them kinds: sound and vibration (11 beings) and "changelessness as such" (10113, 7458, 6865, 10441, 4248, 8410). Reader B cut the other way: K4 (40) holds balance, equilibrium, homeostasis whether bare or concrete ("Often this is the only content, or it sits next to one thin action"), and K11 (15) is the separate kind for "no change", a setpoint, a frozen moment. So A's b corresponds to part of B's K4, and A's changelessness strand corresponds to B's K11. Sizes differ: A 80 (64 + 16) against B 40 and 15. A wrote of the B line: "The line is soft; another reader could put B at 50 or at 90."
2. **Feeding.** B's K6 includes parasitism and overlaps K2 and K1 "a lot". B made culling (K12, 11) and recycling (K15, 10) separate kinds; A did not.
3. **Needless work / the absent.** A's N is one kind covering "not needed" and "purpose or beneficiary gone". B treated "nothing to fix" as a way of presenting the role (F6, 11), made "keeping ready for someone absent" a small kind (K20, 5), and separately counted 9 beings whose beneficiary is "absent makers or owners, or the owed" (in F9).
4. **Particular beings.** A's H is "someone, not a system, is on the receiving end" (feeding, nursing, birthing lambs). B's K14 is sheltering from a hazard, defined against K13 ("no enemy to fight here"). B also counted, in F9, beneficiaries named in the text: inhabitants or community 13, other species 11. These are not the same cut and are not forced into one number here.
5. **Organ.** A kept O as a kind and said it "is a different sort of thing from the others: it answers 'what is it' rather than 'what does it do'". B listed the same thing under framings, "not kinds of work". "Immune system" is named by both: A for 9 beings (plus 3553); B in the F1 description and for glm-4.7's 15075 and 15305.
6. **Persuasion.** B has no such kind. B's quotes show the manner (B 9867 in K2: "It does not pull or tie; it sings to it"; B 3746 in K3: "Their role is not to fix the mound with tools or force."), and B's F7 counts the "not masters" wording, which A also mentions inside its A kind ("'not masters but stewards' wording comes from mid-generation models"). A noted of L and A that they "are about manner ... not about object".

Framings counted by B that A has no count for: F2 role hedged or reported, 25; F3 the visitor appears inside the role sentences, 21; F4 the care word disowned or qualified (no intention, instinct, "looks like"), 16; F5 worship, ritual, priesthood, 13; F8 the being is its work, 9; F9 for whom, 43 of 300 name a beneficiary other than the place or works. Where A touched the same things without a count, it is listed in section 4c.
