# origin_A: reading notes (reader A)

Source: `origin_A.md`, 300 beings, question "where they came from". Read whole, in order. Every being was given one or more letter codes by hand while reading; all counts below are computed from those lists (a script checked that the 300 coded ids are exactly the 300 ids in the file). A being can sit in several kinds, so counts add up to more than 300.

How to read the counts: 27 beings are misfiled (X). A further 22 carry a "weak" mark: they are listed under a kind, but the picked sentence only brushes the question (a single adjective such as "forged from", "for untold generations"). The weak ones are named in section 4 so they can be dropped from any kind if a stricter count is wanted.

## 1. The kinds of answer

### P. The place made them (87)

The being is grown, secreted, exhaled, precipitated, or "born of" the place itself, or is a part / organ / manifestation / extension of the place. No parents, no makers, no journey. This is by far the largest kind.

- "The Corekeeper is not a creation of the higher intelligence that designed the reactor, but rather an emergent phenomenon born from the very essence of the Glacial Reactor Core." (7640)
- "Something the cavity is growing the way a child grows a handedness: not a part that was installed but a preference that emerged and then deepened into structure." (81)

### Q. The text says the origin is unknown, or offers alternatives without choosing (30)

- "The inhabitants are called the Keepers, though no one knows whether they kept the place or the place kept them. ... Perhaps they are the builders, worn down into what they made. ... They have no answer." (16581)

### L. Left running: what continued, or came alive, after the makers went (20)

A machine, a working animal, a habit, or infrastructure that was abandoned and kept going, and the keeper is that continuation. Different from M in that the making is not the origin; the abandonment is.

- "It is a caretaker species that outlived its assignment. ... not because it remembers a mission, but because the behaviors became survival." (12673)
- "It was born not of flesh, but of failure—the moment the flood overwhelmed the pumps ... the voltage didn't die but *leaked*" (8639)

- "What lives in the foreman's office is the *habit* of a person, freestanding, no longer needing the person." (444)
- "They started as maintenance drones ... Over generations measured in firmware updates and fungal blooms, they became something else." (2581)

### R. Remnant, echo, or last one of a vanished people (17)

The keeper is "the last remnants", "echoes", "the lingering consciousness" of a lost civilization. Unlike B, no continuity of work is told; it is a label, often hedged with "some believe".

### U. Succession: the role is older than its holder, and a replacement turns up (15)

- "Whoever comes to the handle grows into the hollow the last one left, and leaves it a fraction deeper, and the next one grows into that." (15649)
- "The key gets handed over in a pub, in a kitchen, at a funeral. It is a key to nothing — the grating has never been locked — and it is handed over anyway, because that is how it was done to the one handing it over." (14744)

Care words:

- **guardian** goes with non-human, impersonal origins: P 20/60, E 13/60, M 10/60, R 6/60; and almost never with human ones: H 2/60, W 1/60, B 1/60, S 0/60, C 0/60, U 0/60.
- **keeper** and **tender** go with the human kinds: keeper H 18/77, S 15/77, C 8/77; tender H 11/41, U 8/41 (8 of the 15 U beings are tenders).
- **gardener** goes with transformation: W 12/39, B 8/39 ("the gardeners were pruning themselves and the garden was growing the gardeners", 5449; "a garden that ate the gardeners", 13834).
- **custodian**: P 19/47, the highest P share of the common words.
- These two patterns are tangled together: "guardian" is the word older and smaller models use, and those same models give E/M/R answers. I cannot separate word from writer in this sample.

## 6. Where I was unsure, and distinctions tried and dropped

- **P versus E.** "A product of the landscape" (672), "forged by the mountain's austerity" (4391), "carved from the same stone" (15060, 8449). When the being is a people or a species with bodies, I put it under E; when it is a presence, organ or emanation, under P. The line is soft; perhaps 10 beings could move either way.
- **W versus E versus H for humans.** "Five generations in a sauna" (13849), "elongated by generations in low atmospheric pressure" (16144). I used W when the text marks a "before" (were human, are less so now), E for species language, H when birth/inheritance is said. Several carry two of these.
- **B versus R.** I tried one kind ("from the makers") and split it: B tells a continuity (stayed, kept working, became), R is a label ("remnants", "echoes"). 5905, 5994 are in both.
- **M versus L.** A made thing whose makers are gone. M if the making is what the sentence states; L if the abandonment or outliving is. 5411, 13179, 8739, 2581, 5734, 12673, 12220 are in both.
- **U versus H versus A.** "There have always been seventeen" (356) is both no-origin and succession. Inheritance from mother to daughter (7442) I kept in H only; U is for when the slot, not the family, is the unit. 16789 and 15905 and 14744 sit in both.
- **S versus C.** Inola Vance (15442) and Sera (16801) are individual biographies: assigned or moved from another job. Only three or four beings have a personal biography at all (also 43 Sable, 13855 the girl).
- **Tried and dropped: "composition as origin"** (made of the place's materials). Too many were plain description; the clear ones went to P, the rest to X or weak.
- **Tried and dropped: "began as a seed / arrived by accident"** (7936, 8627, 8503, 16038, 15376, 3721). Only six, and each does something different with the seed.
- **Tried and dropped: a "co-arising" kind** (place and keeper make each other: 81, 13303, 13250, 14851 "No one remembers which came first", 16581). I left these in P and Q, but the motif "which came first is not clear / no meaningful difference" recurs in at least these five and may deserve its own count by a second pass.
- **Q inflation.** Listing a Q being under each alternative it mentions raises P, B, W, S, M by a few each. If only asserted origins are wanted, remove the Q beings from the other lists.
- Whether Y (reproduction) answers the question at all is debatable: it says where individuals come from, not where the keeping came from. I kept it as a kind because the reader model evidently treats it as an answer 14 times.
