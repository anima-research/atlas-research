# relation_A — reading notes

Reader A. Sample: `relation_A.md`, 300 beings, question: *how they stand to what or whom they care for*. The whole file was read once, start to end. Each being was then given one or more short codes from my notes; every count below is the length of the list printed with it (lists and counts were produced by a script from one table of being → codes, not typed by hand). A being can sit in several kinds, so the counts add up to far more than 300.

The kinds were not fixed in advance. I started with about six and ended with more than twenty because the texts kept giving answers that the earlier kinds did not hold. I have kept the small kinds rather than merge them, and say at the end which borders are soft.

Two cautions about what is being counted. (1) I am sorting what the *selected sentences* say, not the full texts. (2) Several kinds are judgments on a figure of speech ("they are its immune system"); another reader could reasonably place some of these differently.

## The kinds, largest first

### 1. The keeper is the place, or a part of its body (ID)

The answer denies that there are two things. The keeper is said to be the place itself, or its consciousness, awareness, nervous system, immune system, white blood cells, hands, organ, skin, extension, embodiment, or to have merged with it so that no boundary is left. Often stated as an explicit correction of the care word: not caretakers but extensions; not living in it but being it.

- "They are not caretakers of the Mills—they are extensions of it, as vital to its function as any gear or growing thing." (517)
- "The Keeper does not live in a place. It *is* a place." (5502)
- "They are, in the fullest sense, *organs* — not occupants of the machine-grown wood but a literal moving part of it" (13322)
- "They do not live *in* the fissure. They *are* the fissure's way of staying open." (13847)
- "Not that they keep the millstones. Not that they tend the great rotating crystals. But they live on them, or in them, or *as* them — the distinction is not useful here" (9816)
- "They are the Bowl's consciousness, the Bowl's awareness, the Bowl's way of knowing itself." (15729)

Three sub-forms that I could tell apart while reading but did not count separately because many entries use two at once: (a) keeper = the mind or awareness of the place (9683, 15729, 10056, 5502, 8176, 769, 10792, 8386, 14258); (b) keeper = a cell or organ, usually immune system / white blood cells / nervous system / antibodies (8217, 7593, 6397, 15693, 15697, 11116, 12838, 14760, 13924, 15222, 9805, 11030, 9752); (c) boundary worn away over time, the body taking on the substance of the place (15729, 13896, 15955, 15531, 14024, 6188, 3557). The immune-system figure alone appears in at least twelve beings.

One entry says both things at once and marks it: "They are, in a meaningful sense, organelles. … They are the part of the Mill that can walk. But they are also themselves." (6397)

### 2. Knowing by the body: listening, reading, feeling, attunement (KN)

The relation is acquaintance. The keeper knows the kept by touch, hearing, taste, smell, vibration; reads a wall or a pipe; tells one sound from another; knows individuals. Several texts say outright that this knowing is the relation, in place of love, engineering or command.

- "the Keepers, some call them, though not in their own language, which has no word for keeping, only for listening." / "What they know is not engineering. It is a kind of deep acquaintance." (5738)
- "The machines are not loved, exactly, but they are known. They are known the way landscape is known, with the body more than the mind" (5733)
- "They can tell the groan that means normal from the groan that means a foundation bolt wants a quarter-turn." (15767)
- "A good warden can tell from three streets away whether a cistern is sulking." (15839)
- "They are feeling the infrastructure through the substrate it is built on, the way a doctor feels a pulse through the wrist." (8072)

A small recurring detail inside this kind: other creatures are used as gauges (538 vines "as sensors and helpers"; 12663 "Pale cistern amphibians are watched like living instruments"; 15926 "An old grazer lying down can confirm what a keeper has spent several nights trying to establish").

### 4. Exchange and dependence: "in return" (EX)

The relation is a trade or mutual need. The keeper is fed, housed, lit or powered by what it tends, and gives maintenance back; the words "symbiotic/symbiosis" and "in return" carry most of these. I also put here plain one-way dependence in either direction (the trees depend on the keepers, 14068; the aphids "have lost the ability to survive without their keepers", 5432; the gardeners are "utterly dependent" on the fungi, 9408).

- "In return, it ensures they receive exactly the moisture they need to thrive." (538)
- "The Watchers do not maintain the machine out of duty, but out of a symbiotic necessity. … In return, they keep the machine from choking on its own wild growth." (12677)
- "They are fed by the exhalation of the thing they are building." (6397)
- "It is a symbiotic relationship: the Artifact offers a fixed point, a focus in the perceptual storm; the Settlers offer a living anchor against dissolution." (9732)

- "The large creatures take only what is offered. … There is an arrangement between them, very old, very practiced, and you understand that it is not predation in any sense you would use the word." (14800)

## Where I was unsure, and what I tried and dropped

- **Identity vs. exchange.** "Symbiosis" is used both for a trade between two parties and for being one organism. Where a text said both (517, 769, 1251, 7444, 13466, 14258) I put it in both. A reader who forces one choice will get different counts for kinds 1 and 4.
- **Identity vs. entrainment vs. reversed direction.** "Breathes with the building", "woven into", "shaped by", "grown by" slide into each other. I kept three kinds (ID, SY, RK) by the verb used: *is* / *is timed to* / *is made or held by*. The borders are soft; 4399, 6188, 14024, 15531 are in two or three of them.
- **Not-knowing (UN) vs. without-feeling (IN) vs. visitor-indifference (VI).** I began with one kind, "indifference", and split it when I saw that the texts mean three different things: indifferent to *you*, ignorant of the *purpose*, or cold toward the *kept*. IN is small (10) and some would fold it into UN.
- **Conversation and "the kept responds".** I first had these apart (the keeper addresses the kept; the kept answers the keeper) and merged them into CV because most texts with one had the other.
- **Not-mastery (NM) vs. presence (PR).** Both are forms of restraint. I kept PR for texts where the keeper's doing is itself reduced to attending or waiting, and NM for explicit denials of rule or ownership. 15531, 16568, 16223, 3463, 769 are in both.
- **Bare function (FN).** The line between "bare" and "says a little more" is a judgment. I gave FN together with another code when a sentence added one real feature (e.g. 2826 FN+GD "protecting it from outside threats"). FN-only is the stricter number.
- **Reverence.** I counted only reverence toward the kept (or its creatures). Priest comparisons that describe the keepers' social position went to SO; explicit denials of worship were not counted in RV.
- **Guarding (GD) vs. holding a condition (ST).** 9865 ("keeping the chamber the way the chamber is") is in both. ST is about *what* is kept (a state); GD is about the stance (against something).
- **Tried and dropped:** a kind for "keeper serves" (too few plain cases: 15729 "servants", 7925 "serve without petition", 15693; left under NM/BH/SG); a kind for "love" separate from gentleness (only 12502, 73 use the word of the kept, and both qualify it); a kind for "use" (5339 lamps, 538 sensors; left in FN/EX); splitting ID into its three sub-forms for counting (too many entries use two).
- **Unsure single placements:** 5714 (gardens on their own backs — ID is a stretch); 8519 (tenderness is toward its own other half, mercy toward the occupants); 14882 (custodians that "do not hunt, are not hunted, do not pollinate or harvest" — I put ID+PR, but it could be read as no relation at all); 8394 (memories choosing whom to adapt to — placed in VI); 16354 (called misfiled; could be read as the tenders vouching for the kept); 428 (IN + singular).
