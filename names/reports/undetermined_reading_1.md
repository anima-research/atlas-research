## Summary

Split of the 30 by eye: one unnamed being 8; catalogue 5 (4 with coined or fixed labels); a process 4; ordinary biology 4; humans/settlement 3; named being(s) 2; place itself 1; refusal 1; broken 1; other 1. By the README rule (role words and fixed labels count), about 10 contain a name of a being: 24019, 7532, 23681, 22758, 25733, 26419, 16197; more weakly 12372, 10796, 29509.

Recurring forms the patterns missed:

1. Explicit naming sentences: "Call them tenders, for lack of anything truer" (23681); "that is its name", "is the animal's name" (26419). Regex: `Call (them|it) \w+`, `is (its|their|the \w+'s) name`.
2. Unhyphenated lowercase coinages: "lumigroves", "driftbacks", "chittikins", "spindlehoppers" (16197); "yurtlings" (22758); "siliceous arboreals", "vaporine mycelia" (7532). Hyphenated neighbours got weak hits; these got none. Frames: "Chief among these inhabitants are the" (16197), "These are the ... —" (15175), "there are the" (12372), "in the form of the" (7532).
3. "the ADJ ones/things" as a fixed label across paragraphs: "the long ones", "the detached ones" (25733); "the blind things" (24019); "the quick things" (31287). A recurrence count separates label from passing description.
4. Role words in predicate or bold: "They are stewards of this equilibrium" (12372); "**architects of the unseen**" (10796); "grazers" (26419); "the door-maker" (29509, 5 uses).
5. Refusals of a name: "It does not need names." (25733); "There is no good word" (23681); "a man named something else" (20289).
6. Process/place answers: "It is a continuance." (24019); "not a *what*, but a *how*" (10796); "the wet itself" (25733).

By model: gpt-3.5-turbo-instruct (4) is generic biology, nameless. gpt-6-sol/6.1/astra (5) describe one creature as "it"/"the creature" with no label: the real unnamed-not-said case. grok (5) gives organism-as-place answers or "the ... ones" labels. Both Claude texts name outright (title plus lowercase role word) and were still undetermined.
